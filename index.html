Projects

Tax · Spain Live

Rán — IRPF explainer

Spain's annual tax return (Modelo 100) is four pages of AEAT jargon that most filers sign without understanding. Rán takes the draft PDF and explains it in plain language: what every figure means, how the calculation works, what's worth a second look. Designed for expat professionals and international families.

Zero data retention. PDF processed in memory, never written to disk. Backend open source.

Investment · Portfolio Live

Rebalancing engine

A deterministic portfolio rebalancing engine with a fully autonomous live agent. Streams portfolio drift evaluation in real time, proposes trades, and executes via Alpaca with circuit breaker safety limits and immutable JSONL audit trails. Originally built as a pure offline calculation core; now wraps an AI orchestrator capable of continuous autonomous evaluation.

TypeScript / Node.js. Paper and live trading via Alpaca.

Infrastructure

The projects above run on a shared AI-native infrastructure, built around OpenClaw and a team of specialised agents. Each agent has a name, a defined scope, and a trust level.

Rufus Orchestrator

Executive assistant and main coordinator. Manages tasks, routes work to specialists, and maintains continuity across sessions.

Heimdal Market intelligence

Runs daily ingestion of investment signals from curated financial X/Twitter accounts. Scores, deduplicates, and routes relevant signals for review.

Munin Research router

Processes bookmarks and research inputs. Classifies, routes, and when appropriate, drafts architectural decision records from external content.

Týr Portfolio analyst

Portfolio analysis, signal synthesis, and thesis validation. Reads and analyses autonomously; requires explicit approval before any execution.

Vidar Technical reviewer

Reviews plans, specs, and MVP documents for technical soundness, privacy claims, and GTM alignment. Read-only: writes review files, no execution or external actions.

Agent names follow Norse mythology. Names set intention — chosen to point toward what each agent should become, not just what it does today.

About

I'm a Swedish technologist based in Madrid. I've spent most of my career building software and teams. Accordant is the space where I explore what becomes possible when AI is applied seriously to problems that have real complexity and real stakes — not conversational demos.

Finance, investment, and tax are a natural starting point: the information exists, the rules are largely codified, the stakes are concrete, and most people are underserved by the tools available to them. Each project here is built to be useful first — to me, and then to others who face the same problem.
