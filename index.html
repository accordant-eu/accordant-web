Projects

Tax · Spain Live

Rán — IRPF explainer

Spain's annual tax return (Modelo 100) is four pages of AEAT jargon that most filers sign without understanding. Rán takes the draft PDF and explains it in plain language: what every figure means, how the calculation works, what's worth a second look. Designed for expat professionals and international families navigating cross-border complexity.

Claude (Sonnet 4.6) is the analytical engine. Zero data retention — PDFs are processed in memory, never written to disk. Backend is open source and intentionally short enough to audit in five minutes.

Investment · Portfolio Live

Portfolio rebalancing engine

A deterministic portfolio rebalancing engine that started as an offline calculation core and evolved — through AI-driven development — into a fully autonomous live agent. The engine streams portfolio drift in real time, proposes trades, and executes via Alpaca with circuit breaker safety limits and immutable JSONL audit trails.

Built entirely through AI-assisted development: architecture decisions, implementation, tests, and documentation all produced through structured LLM collaboration following a custom AGENTS.md ruleset and 38+ ADRs. The live agent is fed signals from the broader OpenClaw intelligence harness.

OpenClaw · Agent team

The harness

The projects above share an AI-native infrastructure built around OpenClaw. Rather than a single general-purpose assistant, the setup uses a team of specialised agents — each with a defined scope, trust level, and operating model. Agents handle work autonomously within their brief; anything consequential requires explicit approval.

Rufus Orchestrator

Executive assistant and main coordinator. Manages tasks, routes work to specialist agents, maintains continuity across sessions, and acts as the primary interface for day-to-day work.

Heimdal Market intelligence

Runs daily ingestion of investment signals from curated financial accounts on X. Scores relevance, deduplicates, and routes signals downstream for synthesis and portfolio review.

Munin Research router

Processes bookmarks and research inputs. Classifies content, routes it by topic, and drafts architectural decision records when external content warrants a documented response.

Týr Portfolio analyst

Portfolio analysis, signal synthesis, and thesis validation. Consumes Heimdal's signals and feeds the rebalancing engine. Reads and analyses autonomously — execution requires approval.

Vidar Technical reviewer

Reviews plans, specs, and product documents for technical soundness, privacy claims, and strategic alignment. Read-only: produces review files, no external actions.

Names follow Norse mythology — chosen to set intention, not just label a function. The naming philosophy: pick a name that points toward what an agent should become, not just what it does today.

Philosophy

The approach

Each project here treats AI as a first-class collaborator, not a productivity tool. The rebalancing engine's entire architecture — decisions, tests, documentation, implementation — was produced through structured LLM collaboration. Rán uses Claude as its analytical engine. The OpenClaw harness runs agents that monitor markets, route research, and review code autonomously.

The consistent pattern across projects: deterministic, auditable logic at the core, with AI handling the reasoning layer above it. Not AI generating code that humans maintain — AI as an ongoing operator of systems that remain legible and verifiable.

Finance and tax are a natural starting point. The rules are largely codified. The stakes are real. The gap between what professionals have access to and what individuals have access to is large. And the problems are concrete enough that you know immediately whether the output is any good.
