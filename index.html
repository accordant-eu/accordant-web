About

I'm a Swedish technologist based in Madrid. I've spent most of my career building software and teams. Accordant is the space where I explore what becomes possible when AI is applied to problems that are genuinely hard — not just conversational demos, but things with real complexity and real stakes.

Finance and tax are a natural starting point: the information exists, the rules are codified, the stakes are concrete, and most people are chronically underserved by the tools available to them.

Each project here is built to be useful first — to me, and then to others who face the same problem.
